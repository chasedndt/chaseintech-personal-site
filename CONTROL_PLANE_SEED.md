## Product framing

ChaseInTech Personal Site is the public developer/founder homepage and builder router for Chase / ChaseInTech.

It replaces the current Carrd site:

- https://chaseintech.carrd.co/

Future domain target:

- https://chaseintech.com/

This is broader than a portfolio. It should explain who Chase is, what he is building, and where visitors should go next.

The site should route visitors into:

- ChaseOS
- Chaser Agent
- StrikeZone
- writings / updates
- demos / experiments
- business-facing build surfaces
